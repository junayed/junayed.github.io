Map: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Pick a place to focus on it.

Roam: follow-camera view with a walker. WASD to move, drag to turn, scroll for camera distance. Shopfronts and street furniture are game-style interpretations.

Walk: WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift to run, drag to look. Buildings, water, parked cars and physical obstacles block walking and running. Bridges remain crossable. The walker and camera follow hills.

Fly: WASD to fly, Q/E to descend/rise, Shift to move faster, drag to look. On a phone, the joystick travels level while ↑ / ↓ change altitude; Fast changes speed. You can look and move with different fingers. Flying has no collision boundary.

Sunny day: choose Sunny day for bright sunshine, white clouds, gulls and sparkling water. Night reveals the Christmas village, festive shopping-street lights and colourful Codona’s. Street voices optionally enables installed local speech voices; greetings also appear as captions.

City life: Quiet, Everyday or Busy changes commuter and traffic density. People, Traffic and Shop signs have separate toggles. Look for Street life in Places to meet buskers, dog walkers, shoppers and café visitors, or find red post boxes, telephone kiosks and open parking decks. Shop names are fixed to the mapped façades, including beach businesses.

Roadworks: City life → Roadworks switches on designed work sites with cones, amber beacons, barriers and footpath detours. Work zones block walkers; switching off removes those barriers. Search Places for Works or Refuse collection. These scenes are illustrative, not current road-closure information. Bins remain in the city; refuse trucks and crews follow Traffic and People.

Hills & playgrounds: search Places for Hills, Cobblestones or Play Park. Explore downhill streets, textured sand and grass, and children playing on swings and roundabouts. Playground visitors follow the People switch and become quiet at night.

Use Map, Roam, Walk and Fly to switch views at the selected place. Press R to return to that place.

This is a game-style map interpretation of the selected city and coastal area. It uses real mapped outlines; it is not Google’s exported city mesh. Terrain uses cached approximate Mapzen elevation data. Road grades and bridge decks are interpreted. Building heights are inferred from mapped storeys where available, otherwise estimated. The overhead images are projected onto flat terrain and roofs; this is approximate, not a surveyed orthophoto. Marischal College, Union Square and the Town House have dedicated architectural interpretations. Other façades, shops, street furniture and vehicles are simplified. Bus stops, crossings and points of interest use mapped locations. Signals, traffic, bus liveries, schedules, shops and street details are simplified game interpretations; this is not live traffic or a travel planner.
